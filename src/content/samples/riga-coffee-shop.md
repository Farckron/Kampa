## Positioning

Rīts is the 90-second coffee stop for people who walk to work in the Miera iela quarter, not a laptop cafe and not a destination brunch spot. The promise is narrow and defensible: doors open at 07:30, your drink is on the counter before 09:00 no matter the queue, and by the third visit the barista starts it when you walk in. Everything below is built to convert "I passed that place" into "that is my morning place" inside three weeks of a person's first visit.

## Who we're talking to

The primary target is a 28 to 45 year old who works within a 700 metre walk: the design and IT offices in the Tallinas iela quarter, the Laima factory offices at Miera iela 22, the clinics and law offices on Ģertrūdes and Bruņinieku. They arrive on foot or off tram 11 between 08:20 and 09:15, buy one drink (cappuccino €3.20 or filter €2.80), roughly 40% add a pastry, and they are price-insensitive but ferociously time-sensitive. They already have a default coffee habit, either Caffeine on Čaka or the office machine, so we are not creating demand, we are stealing a 12-visit-per-month routine.

Secondary: the remote workers and freelancers of the quarter who come 10:00 to 12:00, sit for 40 minutes, and spend about €6.50. Worth having, but they are not the goal and they are not who the 30 days is aimed at. If a tactic serves only them, it gets cut.

## Channel decisions

**Instagram.** Nearly everyone in the 700 metre catchment who has not yet walked in has seen the street, so the job is not awareness, it is giving them a reason to break their existing routine on one specific morning. Boosted posts at a 3 km radius let us put a dated, concrete offer ("filter coffee €1.50 before 09:00, Tuesday to Thursday") in front of about 9,000 locals for €35 a push, which is the cheapest way we have to buy a first visit.

**Google Business Profile.** "Coffee near me" and "kafejnīca Miera iela" are searched by exactly the person who is already on the street with 10 minutes to spare, and the profile is free real estate we currently under-use: 11 photos, no opening post, no menu with prices. Fixing the profile, posting weekly, and pushing reviews from 62 to 72 moves us up the map pack, which is the only channel here that keeps working after the budget stops.

**Email.** A morning habit is a repetition problem, not a discovery problem, so we need a channel we own to nudge someone back on week two and week five without paying for reach again. A counter signup tied to the coffee card gives us a list of people who have already paid us once, which is the highest-intent audience we will ever have and costs €0 to mail on MailerLite's free tier under 1,000 subscribers.

**Rejected:**

- **TikTok.** The reach is national and mostly under 25, and a coffee shop with 20 seats cannot serve reach it cannot walk to. Also costs 3 to 5 hours a week of a 4 hour budget to do it non-embarrassingly.
- **Paid search (Google Ads).** Riga coffee keywords are cheap but the volume in a 1 km radius is a few hundred searches a month, and the organic map pack already captures them once the profile is fixed. We would be paying for clicks we can have for free.
- **LinkedIn.** The audience is right and the format is wrong: nobody chooses a €3.20 cappuccino from a feed they open on Sunday evening. Office reach is bought better with 200 flyers handed to six reception desks.
- **A delivery app listing (Wolt/Bolt Food).** Commission of 25 to 30% on a €3.20 drink kills the margin, and delivered coffee cannibalises exactly the walk-in habit we are trying to build.
- **Local influencer collabs.** €150 to €250 per post in Riga for an audience that is city-wide and brunch-shaped, with no way to attribute a single 08:40 weekday visit.

## Copy you can paste

### Instagram post, week 4

We open at 07:30. Not 08:00, not "around eight."

Half of Miera iela is still shuttered when we grind the first batch, and honestly that first hour is the best one: no queue, the machine is warm, and there are usually still almond croissants.

If you walk past us on the way to the tram and you have never come in, this week is the week. Filter coffee is €1.50 before 09:00, Tuesday to Thursday. That is it, no card needed, no app.

I will be the one behind the machine.

### Instagram post, week 4 — tasting day

Tomorrow morning there is a jug of Ethiopian filter by the door and it is free.

We bought 3 kg of a washed Guji from a roaster in Tartu and it tastes like apricot and black tea, which sounds like something people say about coffee to sound clever, but stand at the counter and you will get it in one sip.

08:00 to 09:30, until it runs out. Take the 60 ml, tell me if you hate it, that is genuinely useful to me.

Thursday same again if there is any left.

### Email 1, sent week 4

You picked up a coffee card from us, so you get this once a month. It is short, and unsubscribing is one click at the bottom, no hard feelings.

We open at 07:30 because I used to work in an office on Ģertrūdes and there was nowhere to get a decent coffee before the meeting nobody wanted to be in. Twenty seats, one machine, me and one other person. That is the whole operation.

This month we are pouring a washed Guji from Ethiopia as filter, €2.80, and the usual Brazil and Colombia blend for milk drinks. If you take milk, the flat white is the one I would order.

Two practical things:

The card only stamps before 11:00. That is deliberate. Mornings are what we are good at and what we are trying to protect.

If you are ever in a hurry, say so at the door. We will start your drink before you reach the till. Regulars do this and it takes about 90 seconds.

See you Tuesday,

Ilze
Rīts, Miera iela

## How you'll know it's working

**1. Weekday morning transactions, 07:30 to 10:30.** Baseline is 41 a day, averaged over four weeks. Target by week 4 is 51 a day, which is the +25%. Check it in the POS: export the daily Z-report, filter to Monday through Friday and that time window, and paste the number into one spreadsheet every Monday. This is the only KPI that decides whether the 30 days worked.

**2. Google Business Profile direction requests and reviews.** Direction requests are running at 45 a month; target 80 by week 4. Reviews are at 62 with a 4.6 average; target 72 reviews with the average holding at 4.5 or better. Both are in the Business Profile performance tab, checked every Monday. Direction requests are the closest free proxy we have for "someone decided to walk here."

**3. Coffee card redemption rate.** Target: 500 cards issued, at least 15% reaching stamp 10 inside the 30 days, so 75 or more redemptions. Count the physical completed cards in the till drawer at the end of each week. Below 10% means the offer is not strong enough to change a routine; above 25% means we are giving away margin to people who were already coming, and the threshold moves to 12 stamps.

Secondary, watched but not steered by: email list size (target 60 subscribers by week 4) and open rate (target 45% or better, which is realistic for a list that opted in at a counter).
